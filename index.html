Sample Day

What a day with Sam looks like

Here's an example plan for each trip. Change anything you like. It's your journey, your way.

  1. MorningHotel pickup

    Sam collects you from your hotel and you head south on the expressway.

  2. Late morningCoastal stops

    Break the journey at a quiet beach or a turtle hatchery along the coast.

  3. MiddayGalle Fort

    Walk the ramparts, see the lighthouse and wander the colonial streets.

  4. AfternoonLunch & free time

    Seafood lunch, cafés and boutique shopping inside the fort.

  5. EveningReturn

    Relax in the air-conditioned car on the way back to your hotel.

FAQ

Good to know

How much does a day trip cost?

Prices depend on the destination, distance, number of passengers and the stops you'd like. Send Sam a message with your plans and you'll get a clear quote with no surprises.

Where can you pick us up?

Sam can pick you up from your hotel or guesthouse in Negombo, Colombo and nearby areas, or directly from Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB).

Can we change the itinerary?

Absolutely. Itineraries are fully flexible. Add a stop, stay longer somewhere you love, or skip what doesn't interest you. It's your journey, your way.

Do you offer trips longer than one day?

Yes. Ask about multi-day private tours covering places like Sigiriya, Ella, Nuwara Eliya, Yala and the south coast.
